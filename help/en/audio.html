Audio

Source audio

Use the speaker button on the source audio track to turn it on or off. When off, it is left out of the export too. Mute and volume on the transport bar affect the preview only.

Effect audio

  • File › Add Effect Audio (at Frame 0)… (Ctrl+Alt+O)
  • File › Add Effect Audio (at Current Frame)… (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+O)
  • The buttons in the add row at the bottom of the timeline do the same. You can add several tracks; they are mixed in playback and export.

Editing a track

  • Drag the purple handles at the file's ends to change the offset.
  • Click a section to open its menu, where you can split or delete it.
  • Drag a whole section to move it, and drag its edge to change its length.
  • Use the track's [⋯] menu to type values or close the track. To close them all, use File › Close All Effect Audio.
