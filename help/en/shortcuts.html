F1User guide

Mouse

  • Wheel over a viewer: zoom. Middle button or Alt+drag: pan.
  • Over the timeline, Ctrl+wheel: zoom. Wheel or Shift+wheel: scroll left and right. Alt+wheel: scroll the object rows.
  • Keypad 4, 6, 8, 2: scroll a zoomed viewer (with Shift, one screen at a time).
