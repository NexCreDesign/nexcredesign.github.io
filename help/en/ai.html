AI Connection

You can ask an AI such as Claude Desktop to edit for you. Its changes appear on screen at once, and Ctrl+Z undoes them.

Connecting

  1. Check that Settings › Allow AI connection (MCP) is on. It is on by default.
  2. Add the line below to mcpServers in Claude Desktop's claude_desktop_config.json.
  3. Quit Claude Desktop completely and open it again.
"effectvideo": { "command": "effectvideo-mcp" }

When connected, "AI connected" appears at the bottom of the window. If the app is closed, it starts on the AI's first request.

Things to try

  • "Open this video as the source and this one as an effect, then delay the effect by 12 frames"
  • "Put 'Hello' in the top right from 3 to 5 seconds"
  • "Show me the current frame"
  • "Undo that"
  • "Export as H.265"

Good to know

  • One Ctrl+Z undoes one batch of changes sent by the AI. If the AI splits your request into several batches, press it that many times. Opening a source or project, and opening or closing an effect video, cannot be undone.
  • The AI needs your permission to drop unsaved work or overwrite a file.
  • While you are dragging on screen, the AI's request waits until you finish. Edit requests are refused during an export.
  • An export started by the AI closes its window when done, and the AI tells you the result.
  • Only connections from this PC are accepted. Frames you show the AI are sent to that AI service.
  • In the free edition, videos exported by the AI also carry the watermark.
